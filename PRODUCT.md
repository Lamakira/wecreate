# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

WeCreate serves people and organizations in Benin looking for premium video production, and creators who buy its digital filmmaking products. The transaction prototype focuses on guests buying ebooks and LUTs from a mobile or desktop browser, often over a mobile connection.

## Product Purpose

WeCreate presents cinematic production work, turns service interest into a direct conversation, and sells Digital Products with immediate online payment and protected delivery. A successful digital purchase leaves the buyer certain about payment status, email delivery, access duration, and remaining downloads.

## Positioning

WeCreate applies the same authored, cinematic point of view to client work and to the practical tools it sells to creators: “Premium · Cinématographique · Différent.”

## Operating Context

The site is French-first and prices Digital Products in whole XOF/FCFA amounts. Guests check out with a full name, email, and international telephone number, accept the effective CGV and digital-delivery terms, then pay through a hosted FedaPay flow. Services remain outside website commerce and route to WhatsApp or an external Calendly page.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The Digital Cart contains distinct ebooks and LUTs only.
- Payment truth and Fulfillment State remain independent.
- The browser return never claims success until payment is verified.
- Order Access lasts 30 days and grants up to five downloads per Digital Product through short-lived links.
- A failed or cancelled payment may be retried while its Order Snapshot remains active for 24 hours.
- A changed price must be acknowledged before checkout continues.
- No customer accounts, coupons, gifting, subscriptions, postal address, marketing opt-in, service deposits, or fiscal invoices are in scope.
- Product prices in the current brief are working values awaiting client confirmation.

## Brand Commitments

The name is WeCreate. Confirmed visual commitments are a pure black-and-white palette, square corners, Playfair Display headings, Inter interface copy, an editorial cinematic rhythm, and French copy that is premium and direct. Transaction screens must be quiet and light on bandwidth.

## Product Principles

- Never confuse payment approval with fulfillment success.
- State the current transaction truth before offering the next action.
- Keep service conversations and Digital Product commerce visibly separate.
- Make reassurance work on narrow screens and slow connections.
- Treat access rules as customer guidance, not technical implementation detail.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-first responsive behavior, keyboard access, visible focus, reduced-motion safety, and WCAG 2.2 AA are required. Pending payment must explain that the page can be closed safely because confirmation and delivery continue by email.
