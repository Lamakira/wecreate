# Keep service enquiries outside website commerce

Version one will send Service Enquiries to a prefilled WhatsApp conversation or an external call-scheduling flow, preserving WeCreate's existing conversation and payment process. FedaPay, order persistence, fulfillment, and the protected commerce back office therefore apply only to Digital Products; this supersedes ADR-0002 and ADR-0004, trading on-site service conversion tracking and deposit automation for a simpler experience aligned with current operations.
